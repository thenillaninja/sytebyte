01

Getting Started

SyteByte Consult helps turn business observations into a practical action plan. The core workflow is Intake → Review → Analyze → Prioritize → Present.

Each consultation is saved locally as you work. You can move backward through completed stages without losing information, and previously reached stages remain available from the workflow bar.

02

Choose Your Mode

New consultations begin by asking how SyteByte Consult will be used.

Business Owner

Choose Review my own business for a guided experience with simpler prompts and self-assessment language.

Consultant

Choose Review a client business for the professional consultation workspace with faster, consultant-focused wording.

Both modes use the same underlying workflow and converge into the same Analyze, Prioritize, and Present stages.

03

Intake

Capture the business name, industry, website, location, and the main goal or reason for the consultation.

In Business Owner mode, the prompts are framed around what the owner would like to improve. In Consultant mode, the same information is captured as a professional intake.

04

Review

Review the current state of the business across areas such as Website, Google Presence, Lead Capture, Social Presence, and Trust Signals.

Select the status that best describes each area and add a quick observation when useful. Review is meant to record what exists today, not diagnose the problem yet.

Items marked as needing work or missing are carried forward into Analyze as draft findings.

05

Analyze

Analyze turns observations into clear findings and recommendations.

Each finding can include the original evidence, why the issue matters to the business, and a recommended action.

Refine

Use Refine to improve the wording of a finding title.

Draft Impact

Use Draft Impact to create a starting point for explaining why the finding matters.

Draft Action

Use Draft Action to create a suggested next step. Drafted text remains editable and should be reviewed before presentation.

You can also use Add Finding to capture an opportunity that did not originate in Review.

06

Prioritize

Prioritize helps decide what should be acted on first using two simple measures: Impact and Effort.

Higher-impact and lower-effort findings rise toward the top. A high-impact, low-effort item is identified as a Quick Win.

Check Add to action plan for the findings that should appear in the final client report.

07

Present

Present creates the client-facing Business Snapshot & Recommended Action Plan from the information already entered during the consultation.

The report includes the business goal, current-state snapshot, key findings, and selected priorities.

Use Print / Save PDF to create a clean, white-paper-style report. Internal Quick Notes and app navigation are excluded from the printed document.

08

Managing Consultations

Open Consultations to view saved consultation workspaces.

Each saved consultation shows its current stage, number of findings, priorities, and last-updated date.

Available actions include Open, Duplicate, Export, Rename, and Delete.

On smaller screens, use the Consultations button below Business Review to open the slide-out consultation drawer.

09

Import & Export

Use Export to save a consultation as a SyteByte Consult JSON file. This provides a portable backup of the consultation data.

Use Import from the Consultations screen to restore an exported consultation. Imported copies are labeled (Imported) so they can be distinguished from the original.

Duplicate creates a separate editable copy without requiring an export/import cycle.

10

Recommended Workflow

  1. Choose Business Owner or Consultant mode.
  2. Complete Intake.
  3. Review the current state of the business.
  4. Turn relevant observations into findings in Analyze.
  5. Add business impact and recommended actions.
  6. Prioritize findings by Impact and Effort.
  7. Select the items that belong in the action plan.
  8. Review the final report in Present.
  9. Print or save the client report as a PDF.
  10. Export the consultation if you want a portable backup.

Once a stage has been reached, its item in the workflow rail becomes clickable so you can move backward or forward among unlocked stages.

11

Quick Troubleshooting

My consultation disappeared after navigating.

Consultations save automatically in the browser. Open the Consultations screen and select the saved workspace.

I need to move a consultation to another browser or device.

Export the consultation as JSON, then use Import on the other installation.

I cannot reach a later workflow stage.

Future stages remain locked until they have been reached through the normal workflow. Continue from the current stage to unlock the next one.

The PDF looks different from the app.

That is intentional. The PDF uses a clean report layout and removes dashboard containers, app navigation, Quick Notes, and other internal controls.

Will a hard refresh delete my consultation?

A normal refresh does not clear saved consultation data. Clearing browser site data or local storage can remove local consultations, so export important consultations when you want a portable backup.
